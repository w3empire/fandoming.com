Leaked Reports Claim HBO Auditioned Women For Voldemort In Harry Potter TV Reboot; Fans Say ‘This Is Insane’

The latest whispers from Hollywood claim HBO’s Harry Potter reboot is eyeing a woman for the role of the Dark Lord, and the fanbase is already in meltdown.

By Christopher Ivanov
Sep 25, 2025
Summary
  • Rumors claim HBO’s Harry Potter reboot auditioned both men and women for the role of Voldemort, sparking heated debate online.
  • Entertainment insider Daniel Richtman is behind the leak, but his history of unverified scoops has fans questioning the credibility.
  • From Black Snape to possible Lady Voldemort, casting chatter has left Potter fans divided over the reboot’s faithfulness to Rowling’s books.

When Warner Bros. announced plans for a decade-spanning television reboot of the Harry Potter series, the reaction was swift and divided.

Some fans welcomed the idea of revisiting J.K. Rowling’s seven novels with modern production values and a more faithful adaptation.

Others questioned whether the franchise needed a new incarnation so soon after the blockbuster film series concluded in 2011.

The skepticism has only deepened as rumors swirl about potential casting decisions. Early reports suggested that the character of Severus Snape would be portrayed by a Black actor, a change that immediately set off a storm of online commentary.

Now new speculation has emerged, that the producers are considering casting a woman as Lord Voldemort, the saga’s principal villain.

The Source of the Rumor

The claim originated with entertainment insider Daniel Richtman, who has developed a reputation in fan circles for publishing unverified leaks.

Richtman reported that auditions for the role of Voldemort were open to both male and female actors, fueling speculation that HBO might radically reinterpret one of the most infamous characters in modern literature.

From there, the rumor spread quickly across film blogs, YouTube channels, and social media platforms, often accompanied by mock-up images of a feminized Voldemort.

Some posts went further, suggesting that the part had already been cast, but that HBO was withholding the actor’s identity for a dramatic reveal at a later date.

Critics of Richtman note that many of his past “scoops” have proven unreliable, and some outlets, while reporting the claim, were careful to note his spotty track record.

Still, the suggestion of a gender-swapped Voldemort has captured attention precisely because it seems, to some, within the realm of possibility given the changes already rumored for the project.

Fans Split Once More

Reaction to the possibility has been sharp. On online forums dedicated to the franchise, some readers dismissed the story outright as sensationalist clickbait.

Others worried that the reboot was shaping up to be less an adaptation of Rowling’s novels than a reimagining designed to generate publicity through controversy.

“This is ridiculous,” one commentator said in a video responding to the reports. “Based on what we’ve already heard about Snape, anything seems possible. But turning Voldemort into a female character doesn’t make sense from a story perspective.”

While others saw the rumor as an opportunity to critique broader trends in contemporary entertainment.

References to “woke casting” and “virtue signaling” peppered responses from critics who argued that altering the identity of existing characters undermines the narrative integrity of the books.

Supporters of more inclusive casting practices, by contrast, argued that changing a character’s gender or race does not inherently diminish the story, provided the writing remains faithful to its core themes.

Voldemort and the Text

Part of the intensity of the debate stems from Voldemort’s place in the narrative.

Born Tom Marvolo Riddle, the character is depicted in Rowling’s novels as a male wizard whose lust for power drives him to shed his humanity in pursuit of immortality.

By the events of Goblet of Fire, his disfigured form reflects his moral corruption more than any natural human traits.

Some fans have pointed out that the books themselves leave little room for a gender reinterpretation.

Voldemort’s backstory, his troubled relationship with his parents, his time at Hogwarts, and his eventual transformation into the Dark Lord, is heavily gendered in the novels.

There are also canonical references that complicate the idea. In Goblet of Fire, Voldemort famously declares, “I am no man.”

While the line is metaphorical, emphasizing his self-perceived transcendence beyond ordinary human limitations, it has been cited both in jest and in earnest as evidence for or against the plausibility of a female Voldemort.

Industry

The speculation arrives at a moment when major studios are increasingly willing to reinterpret established intellectual property in ways that provoke debate.

Recent years have seen diverse casting choices in franchises from The Little Mermaid to Doctor Who, each of which sparked discussions about representation, fidelity to source material, and the balance between creative license and audience expectations.

For Warner Bros. and HBO, the Harry Potter reboot represents not only a chance to revisit one of the most profitable franchises in history but also a significant risk.

Any casting decision that deviates from the novels is likely to draw scrutiny. At the same time, controversy can generate publicity and keep a series in the public conversation months or years before its release.

The Track Record of Leaks

Richtman’s involvement further complicates the picture. While his reports are widely circulated, many within the industry question their reliability.

Observers note that he often amplifies information already rumored elsewhere, occasionally mixing accurate leaks with speculation.

That track record makes it difficult for fans to discern whether the idea of a female Voldemort reflects genuine casting experiments or simply rumor amplified for engagement.

Some analysts believe the persistence of the rumor speaks less to its accuracy than to the anxieties surrounding the reboot itself.

With every new report, the question of how faithful the series will be to Rowling’s novels becomes sharper.

A Reboot in Question

For many fans, the debate over Voldemort’s gender touches on broader doubts about the project. Will the new series aim to reproduce the novels as faithfully as possible, correcting omissions and compressions from the films?

Or will it use the novels as a jumping-off point for reinterpretation, reshaping the story to appeal to contemporary sensibilities?

If the latter, then radical casting choices might be part of a broader strategy to reframe Harry Potter for a new generation.

If the former, then even rumors of such changes risk alienating audiences before the first episode airs.

Business

The stakes are considerable. Warner Bros. Discovery is banking on the Harry Potter reboot as a pillar of its streaming platform, one that could generate subscription growth over the course of a decade.

The franchise remains enormously lucrative in publishing, theme parks, and merchandise, but its cultural dominance has diminished in recent years.

Complicating matters is Rowling herself, whose public statements on gender identity have polarized opinion.

While she remains financially tied to the franchise, Warner Bros. has sought to distance promotional efforts from her involvement.

A casting controversy centered on gender could therefore take on dimensions far beyond ordinary fan speculation.

The Unanswered Questions

At present, the studio has not confirmed any casting decisions for the reboot. Reports earlier this year suggested that Voldemort had already been cast, though no official announcement has been made.

So the production has indeed auditioned women for the role? That remains unverified.

For now, the idea of a female Voldemort remains just that, a rumor. Yet the intensity of the reaction illustrates the high expectations, and deep anxieties, that accompany any attempt to reimagine a cultural phenomenon as significant as Harry Potter.

Accurate or not, the speculation has already accomplished one thing for HBO, it has ensured that the project remains in the spotlight, with fans and critics alike watching closely for every scrap of news.
