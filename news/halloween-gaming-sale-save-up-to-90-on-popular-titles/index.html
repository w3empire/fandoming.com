Halloween Gaming Sale: Save Up to 90% on Popular Titles

Enjoy up to 90% off games like GTA 5 and Hades on PlayStation, Xbox, and Steam during the Halloween promotions.

By Christopher Ivanov
Oct 17, 2024
Summary
  • Up to 90% off on popular games like Resident Evil 4 Remake and Alien: Isolation across PS5, PS4, Xbox, and Steam.
  • PlayStation offers Armored Core VI: Fires of Rubicon and Hades at discounted prices.
  • Steam users can grab Granblue Fantasy: Relink and GTA 5 for major savings before November 1st.

Granblue, Alien and other games are getting massive price drops this week, with some games slashed by up to 90%. Halloween promotions are in full swing, and you’ve got until November 1st to grab these deals on PlayStation 5 (PS5) and PlayStation 4 (PS4).

Titles like Resident Evil 4 Remake and Alien: Isolation (which, by the way, finally has a sequel coming from Creative Assembly) are leading the charge. Over on Xbox (Series X, Series S, and Xbox One), you’ll find discounts on gems like It Takes Two, Star Wars Jedi: Survivor, and Mass Effect: Legendary Edition.

Over at Steam, the PC gamers aren’t left out either. Games like Granblue Fantasy: Relink, Assassin’s Creed Valhalla, and the good ol’ GTA 5 are up for grabs at sweet prices. Below, you’ll find some of the top deals from this week’s sales:

PlayStation

In addition to the horror classics, Sony’s digital store is throwing in some other big names too. You’ve got titles like Cult of the Lamb, Hades, and Armored Core 6: Fires of Rubicon, yes, that’s the latest one from From Software, the same guys behind Elden Ring. Here’s a list of some standout deals:

  • Resident Evil 4 Remake - $19.95
  • Alien: Isolation - $4.99
  • Cult of the Lamb - $12.45
  • Hades - $9.96
  • Armored Core VI: Fires of Rubicon - $35.99
  • Dead by Daylight - $11.96
  • Lies of P - $35.99
  • Deathloop - $11.99
  • Inscription - $7.96
  • Resident Evil Village - $14.76

Xbox

The Halloween vibes are just as strong on Xbox, with the remake of Dead Space going for less than half price. And if you’ve been eyeing Need for Speed Unbound or Plants vs. Zombies: Garden Warfare, they’re going cheap too. Check out the best Xbox deals here:

  • It Takes Two - $13.93
  • Star Wars Jedi: Survivor - $27.12
  • Mass Effect: Legendary Edition - $8.97
  • Dead Space (Remake) - $23.73
  • Need for Speed Unbound - $10.17
  • Plants Vs. Zombies: Garden Warfare 2 - $4.14
  • F1 24 - $35.90
  • Rayman Origins - $3.99
  • UFC 5 - $35.90
  • Battlefield 2042 - $8.97

Steam

Steam is the place to be if you’re a PC gamer. While we’re still waiting for their big seasonal sale, this week still offers some good picks like Blasphemous and the remastered Tomb Raider IV-VI collection (yes, the classic Lara Croft games). Here are a few highlights from the current sales:

  • Granblue Fantasy: Relink - $23.99
  • Assassin’s Creed Valhalla - $9.99
  • GTA 5 - $16.48
  • Blasphemous - $6.69
  • Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered - TBA
  • F1 24 - $35.90
  • Planet Zoo - $7.62
  • Portal 2 - $0.66
  • Haven - $7.40
  • Tales of Arise - $22.99

If you’ve been holding off on grabbing these titles, now’s your chance. Deals like this don’t stick around forever, and we both know what happens when the clock strikes November 2nd, back to full price. Don’t say I didn’t warn you.
