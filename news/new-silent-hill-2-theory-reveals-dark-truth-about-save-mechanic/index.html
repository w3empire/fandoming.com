New Silent Hill 2 Theory Reveals Dark Truth About Save Mechanic

Reddit user connects red save squares to the supernatural forces in Silent Hill.

By Christopher Ivanov
Oct 21, 2024
Summary
  • Fan theory suggests red save squares in Silent Hill 2 Remake capture James’ soul.
  • Reddit user connects the save mechanic to the town’s supernatural abilities.
  • Bloober Team praised for adding deeper layers to the game’s original mystery.

A Silent Hill 2 Remake player just dropped a pretty wild theory about those eerie red save squares we’ve all been staring at for years. According to this fan, the glowing squares are more than just a game mechanic, they might actually tie into the town’s supernatural vibes.

The theory suggests that the ominous red glow surrounding James, the protagonist, could have a deeper meaning related to Silent Hill’s haunted setting. And honestly? It’s kind of hard to unsee once it’s pointed out. This idea is just another example of how Konami’s twisted storytelling keeps pulling us in.

A fan theory sheds light on Silent Hill 2 Remake’s red save squares, proposing they capture the protagonist’s soul as he progresses through the game.

For those catching up, Bloober Team dropped the 2024 remake of the 2001 PlayStation 2 classic, originally made by Team Silent. The story follows James Sunderland, a grieving widower, back to the fog-covered town where everything went wrong. Silent Hill 2 Remake has stirred up a lot of excitement for the lore, showing just how good Konami is at creating these messed-up worlds.

Meanwhile, Bloober Team is building a pretty solid horror portfolio with hits like Layers of Fear, Blair Witch, and The Medium. And considering the buzz around Silent Hill, it seems they’re becoming a force in the horror game scene.

Now let’s talk about this theory, Reddit user Hydra_Six_Actual posted a deep dive linking Strange Photo #26 to those red save squares. According to them, the save points don’t just save your game, they’re actually taking snapshots of James. The red tint? That’s him staring into the metaphorical lens of a camera. Kinda creepy, right? The theory goes deeper, suggesting the red hue mirrors James’ internal state, reflecting his soul. Strange Photo #26 apparently backs this up with a Polaroid image drenched in red, paired with the cryptic caption, “Shape Forces The Mind.”

SH2R Theory: Is Strange Photo #26 a hint at what the red save squares represent?
byu/Hydra_Six_Actual insilenthill

Silent Hill fans love a good rabbit hole, and this one’s got them hooked. The game’s subreddit is buzzing with discussions about whether these save squares actually capture James’ essence as he spirals through his nightmare. Some think this is how the town can create doppelgangers or mess with reality, like, the game is trapping pieces of James with each save. Honestly, it’s the kind of theory that makes you want to replay the whole game and stare a little closer at those squares.

People can’t help but praise Bloober Team for how much attention they’ve put into these small, haunting details. Even the mannequins in the game have sparked debates about their connection to Mary, James’ wife. It’s pretty clear that Bloober Team didn’t just recreate Silent Hill 2; they added extra layers to the mystery, building on Konami’s original work. And the Silent Hill fandom? They’re still here for it, dissecting every inch of the game and uncovering new secrets with every playthrough.

Who knew red squares could be this creepy, right?
